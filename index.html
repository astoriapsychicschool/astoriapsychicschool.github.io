About me

My name is Jim Flood. I’m a computer programmer — that’s what I do for a living.

I’m also psychic. I have been my whole life, but it wasn’t until my mid-twenties that I learned about psychic reading. Since then, I’ve done many, many readings, as a hobby and for my own personal development.

When I was first looking to learn about this world, others stepped in and taught me. Now I’d like to do the same, and teach what I know.

I won’t try to prove that psychic reading is real, and I’m not the source of all knowledge on psychic reading. I will share what I believe, and what I’ve experienced.

So why practice psychic reading at all? We’re surrounded by a psychic world, in the same way we’re surrounded by nature, by music, by literature, and by the people in our lives. Becoming more aware of that psychic world can be an enriching part of your personal development.
